# SAGA UNBOUND
### A THD Valheim RPG Experience

**Valheim's journey, expanded — not replaced.**

> **Saga Unbound 1.0.1 is released.** The supported installation path is **Hexium → Gale Mod Manager**. Install the pack from [Saga Unbound on Hexium](https://valheim.hexium.gg/mods/THD-ThoseHordeDiks/Saga_Unbound) and allow Gale to resolve the required dependencies automatically.

You know the path: wake beneath the stones, survive the Meadows, push into the Black Forest, brave the Swamp, climb the Mountains, cross the Plains, and sail toward whatever waits beyond.

**Saga Unbound asks what happens when that familiar journey becomes something much bigger.**

Built and balanced by **THD — Those Horde Diks**, Saga Unbound is a curated RPG-focused Valheim experience where classes, magic, expanded equipment, enemies, dungeons, exploration, seasons, settlement systems and quality-of-life mechanics are configured to work together without replacing Valheim's core progression.

This is not a pile of mods dropped into a profile. The configs have been deliberately tuned as a single experience.

> **The world is bigger than you remember.**

## Recommended Mod Manager: Gale

**Gale Mod Manager is the recommended way to install and play Saga Unbound.** The tested 1.0 dependency set uses mods and versions from **both Thunderstore and Hexium**. Gale supports both repositories in the same Valheim profile.

At launch, some of Saga Unbound's tested dependency versions are not available through Thunderstore alone. Managers that only use Thunderstore, including r2modman/Thunderstore Mod Manager, may therefore be unable to reproduce the supported 1.0 profile exactly.

For the intended experience, install Saga Unbound through **Gale** and allow it to install the declared dependencies automatically. Do not manually mix newer/older dependency versions unless you are troubleshooting and understand the consequences.

Official Gale sources: [GitHub](https://github.com/Kesomannen/gale) and [Thunderstore](https://thunderstore.io/package/Kesomannen/GaleModManager/).

> **Fresh Start Recommended:** For the intended Saga Unbound experience, start with a **fresh world and fresh character**. Existing characters are welcome — it's your Viking — but imported progression and endgame equipment can bypass a significant part of the journey. A fresh world is especially recommended so added locations and world content generate as intended.

## Forge Your Own Viking

Saga Unbound expands character identity far beyond a single equipment set. Warrior, archer and summoner paths sit alongside a full magical progression, expanded weapons and armor, Epic Loot enchantments, new utility systems and biome-tiered advancement.

Your build develops as the world does. Powerful options exist, but the pack is configured so that finding a strong item or learning magic does not simply erase the need to progress through Valheim's biomes and bosses.

## Magic, Loot & Character Builds

MagicRevamp, Wizardry and the supporting class systems provide different approaches to magical play rather than one replacement system. Epic Loot adds meaningful item hunting, enchanting and Adventure Mode content while its progression gates remain tied to boss and biome advancement.

Portal magic respects Saga Unbound's teleport restrictions, and summon limits are tuned for multiplayer balance.

![The World Is Bigger Than You Remember](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dedrah-THD/Saga-Unbound/main/artwork/03_World_Bigger_Than_You_Remember.png)

## A World Worth Exploring Again

Saga Unbound is intentionally biased toward **exploration instead of repeatedly farming the closest known location**.

Expanded world locations, ore mines, dungeons, traders, trainers and other discoveries give crews reasons to keep sailing and mapping. Resource and location regeneration is deliberately slower, while settlement automation is kept convenient. The goal is simple: reduce chores at home without removing the reason to leave home.

Dungeons also vary more naturally in scale. Not every crypt or cave is a guaranteed megadungeon; some are modest, while others become locations worth marking and revisiting.

![Conquer Greater Challenges](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dedrah-THD/Saga-Unbound/main/artwork/04_Conquer_Greater_Challenges.png)

## Conquer Greater Challenges

Creature Level and Loot Control, Monstrum, Warfare, Wizardry and the equipment ecosystem create a broader combat curve without discarding vanilla progression. Multiplayer scaling, world-level growth and starred enemies keep an established world dangerous as its Vikings become stronger.

Saga Unbound is not designed around making every fight punishing. It is designed so that progression, preparation, equipment and cooperation continue to matter.

## Survive a Living World

Seasons change the environment and affect survival, farming and exploration. Expanded food, farming and herbalism systems make settlement development more meaningful, while fishing and animal-care additions provide useful passive systems without turning the world into an idle-resource generator.

![Build, Settle, Expand Your Legend](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Dedrah-THD/Saga-Unbound/main/artwork/05_Build_Settle_Expand_Your_Legend.png)

## Build a Settlement, Not a Warehouse Interface

Crafting-from-containers, controlled auto-storage, expanded inventory tools, farming improvements, cooking, ships and building additions are tuned to remove repetitive inventory work while preserving material progression.

Settlement automation is intentionally local. Saga Unbound wants you spending your evening building, fighting, sailing and exploring — not manually moving the same stack of wood between six chests.

## Multiplayer First, Solo Friendly

Saga Unbound was balanced around a small persistent multiplayer group, but nothing prevents solo play. Difficulty systems scale with player count, while classes and equipment provide room for specialized multiplayer roles without requiring them.

## Fresh Worlds, Existing Worlds & Updates

A fresh world is strongly recommended for 1.0. Existing worlds can work, but previously explored regions may not contain newly added world-generation content. Server administrators should always make a complete world backup before installing or updating a large modpack.

Administrative world-upgrade tools are intentionally **not mandatory Saga Unbound dependencies**. Advanced server owners may choose to use tools such as Upgrade World separately when appropriate.

## What Saga Unbound Is Not

Saga Unbound is not intended to trivialize Valheim, replace every vanilla system, hand out unrestricted teleportation, or turn progression into a checklist of overpowered modded gear. It also is not designed around constant dependency churn. Stable, tested releases take priority over updating every dependency the moment a new version appears.
