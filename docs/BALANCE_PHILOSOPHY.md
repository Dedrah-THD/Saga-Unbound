# Balance Philosophy

**Valheim's journey, expanded — not replaced.**

Saga Unbound is built around five principles:

1. **Vanilla progression still matters.** Modded gear, classes and magic expand choices without intentionally bypassing biome/boss advancement.
2. **Exploration should beat repetition.** Slow regeneration and variable dungeon sizes reward finding new places instead of repeatedly farming the closest known resource.
3. **Convenience should remove chores, not adventure.** Storage, crafting and animal-care automation are local conveniences; they do not replace world exploration.
4. **Power should have progression.** Epic Loot, magic, classes and expanded equipment are gated and tuned to grow with the world.
5. **Stability beats update churn.** Saga Unbound updates are curated and tested rather than automatically following every upstream release.
